## Client requirement
TAPASLEARNING requires a single operational workflow that prepares and publishes approved LinkedIn posts and YouTube videos, distributes each published asset through authorized channels, coordinates engagement workflows without fake engagement, and measures progress toward a minimum campaign target of 5,000 YouTube views per video.

## Acceptance criteria (10/10 client scorecard)
- LinkedIn personal post can publish after approval and shows a verified live post URL.
- Company Page publish is enabled only with approved scope and admin permission; otherwise blocked with actionable reason.
- YouTube upload can complete end-to-end after OAuth re-consent, with a real video ID and status.
- Schedule and publish times use the channel/workspace timezone and handle provider errors idempotently.
- ConnectSafely, MagicPost and Supergrow are individually health-checked with verified capabilities.
- vidIQ and TubeBuddy show honest status; no fabricated connected state.
- Each video has a 5,000-view target and source-based 24h/72h/7d progress.
- Engagement tasks are genuine, contextual and user-reviewed; no pods, coordinated likes or unattended comments.

- CI test suite passes and a real runtime deployment is smoke-tested before release is marked production-ready.

## Publication event and automation policy

A verified publication event should create a durable campaign record with:
- platform, asset type, provider post/video/episode ID, canonical URL, publish timestamp and timezone;
- campaign target, UTM URL, channel-specific repurposed copy and creative;
- idempotency key, attempts, provider response, retry state and audit trail;
- next actions: cross-post queue, analytics polling, notification, approved promotion and human engagement review.

**Automatic after verified publication:** analytics polling, target-gap updates, derivative asset generation, distribution scheduling to authorized channels, UTM generation, alerting, comment monitoring where provider APIs allow, drafting suggested replies, and creating a human review inbox.

**Not automatic:** posting comments or likes/reactions on LinkedIn/X/other networks as a growth pod, mass replies, reciprocal engagement, or paid boost spend. Comment/reaction drafts can be generated and queued; a human must review and perform the actual interaction. Paid promotion can be prepared and budget-capped, but campaign launch/spend needs explicit client authorization.

## Client 10/10 release gate
- Every supported publishing channel passes an end-to-end publish or feed-ingestion test.
- Unsupported channels display a truthful blocked/manual status and clear next action.
- Publication event triggers the campaign orchestration once (idempotent).
- Every downstream action has an execution status, retry strategy and audit record.
- Human approval boundaries and spend caps are enforced.
- Metrics are sourced from platform analytics; no fabricated reach/views.
- CI is green and deployment smoke tests are recorded.
